 👋 Hello there! I am Crista Wathen, Ph.D.
 <p>
 🌱 I’m a formally trained archaeologist and a self-taught data scientist who traded my trowel for a computer.

My work has taken me from archaeological research to SaaS, customer success, data analysis, and project management. Along the way, I discovered that I enjoy the same thing in every environment: figuring out messy problems, finding patterns in data, and building practical solutions that make things work better.

I work between people, technology, and data. Whether that means analysing complex datasets, building decision-support tools, working with customers to understand what they actually need, or helping technical teams turn those needs into something usable.

My background in Anthropology also means I’m interested in the why behind the numbers. As I like to say 'Technical tools reveal patters but understanding people  explains the why'.
